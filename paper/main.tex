\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

This is the paper skeleton of the reproduction template that accompanies our
study of reproducibility in quantum computing
research~\cite{koester_repro_2026}: of 127 manually assessed papers, only
24.4\% ship code artefacts, and 64.5\% of those fail to execute in a clean
environment.  The template encodes the recommendations derived there.  Every
number and figure in a paper built from it comes from the pipeline in
\texttt{reproduction/}, following the 1-2-3 reproducibility
guidelines~\cite{mauerer_repro_2022}: one command to build the paper, one to
regenerate the results, and a containerised environment in which both are
known to work.

\Cref{fig:example} is the template's example figure.  It is included with
\verb|\includetikz|, which prefers the TikZ fragment generated by
\texttt{make reproduce} (\texttt{build/plots/}) and falls back to the
committed PDF in \texttt{paper/plots\_precompiled/} when the figure has not
been rebuilt~--~so the paper compiles even without the R toolchain.
